Privacy policy

Bibley. Last updated 9 September 2026.

Bibley is a Bible reading tracker. It keeps what you read, what you write about it, and a small amount of what you choose to share with friends. This page says exactly what is stored, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

What is collected

  • Your email address and name, from Google when you sign in. Sign-in is the only reason they are held, and the name is used to suggest a handle.
  • Your reading. Which chapters you have marked, the day you read each one, an optional time of day, and your reading plan.
  • Your notes and highlights. These are private. They are stored so they reach your other devices and are never shown to anybody else.
  • A profile, only if you choose one. A handle, a display name and an optional photograph. Nothing is published until you pick a handle.
  • What you send to friends. The Bible reference and any message you attach, and a verse you post as your verse of the day.

What is not collected

There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party beacons of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is measured or profiled, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone for advertising. The Bible text itself is served as plain files and reading it reports nothing back.

What friends can see

Only people whose friend request you have accepted, and only a summary the app computes and publishes on purpose: whether you read today, your streak, how far through your plan you are, and the book you are in. Your notes and highlights are never part of it. Choosing "Quiet" in the app publishes nothing except whether you read today.

Anyone signed in who knows your exact handle can see that handle and your display name, which is how somebody adds you. They cannot see anything else without you accepting them.

Who stores it

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • Vercel hosts the app itself.
  • Google provides sign-in, and receives no reading data.

These are service providers. Nobody else receives your data.

Deleting everything

Settings has Close your account. It deletes your account and everything attached to it: reading, notes, highlights, profile, photograph, friendships, messages and posts, on the server and on the device. It is immediate and cannot be undone, so export your journal first if you want a copy. Settings has a button for that too.

Reports made about another account are kept without your name attached to them, so that a complaint does not disappear when somebody closes their account.

Children

Bibley is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions, or anything you want removed: support@bibley.app.
