Community rules

Bibley. Last updated 9 September 2026.

Bibley lets you pick a handle, a display name and a photograph, send a friend a verse with a message, and post one verse a day. Everything in that list is something another person will see, so it comes with rules.

What is not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, or targeting anybody.
  • Hateful content, including anything aimed at a person's race, religion, nationality, disability, sex, gender or orientation.
  • Sexually explicit or violent images or text.
  • Impersonating somebody else, in a name, a handle or a photograph.
  • Spam, advertising, scams, or links sent to people who did not ask for them.
  • Anything illegal.

There is no tolerance for abusive users or objectionable content. Accounts that post it are removed.

What you can do about somebody

  • Block them. Tap the mark on their row in Friends, then Block. They are removed from your friends, their verses leave your screen, and they cannot send you anything or ask to be your friend again. They are not told.
  • Report them. Same menu, then Report. Tell us what is wrong and it reaches the address below.
  • Remove them. Ends the friendship without blocking. They can ask again, so block instead if you would rather they did not.

What happens to a report

Every report is read. We aim to act within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed and the account that posted it can be suspended or deleted. Blocking takes effect the moment you tap it and does not wait for us.

If somebody is in danger, contact your local emergency services. This is a reading app and cannot help with an emergency.
